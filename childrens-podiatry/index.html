Children's Podiatry

Children’s feet and lower limbs are constantly growing, and early issues can often go unnoticed. At The Bowral Foot Clinic, we provide expert paediatric biomechanical assessments to ensure your child’s development stays on track. By identifying and treating foot posture, walking patterns, and muscular imbalances early, we help prevent discomfort, poor coordination, and long-term injury.

What children's do we treat?

  • Flat feet and high arches
  • In-toeing and Pigeon Toe
  • Osgood Schlatters Disease
  • Knee, hip, and lower back pain
  • Toe Walking
  • Sports injuries
  • Walking or gait abnormalities
  • Growing Pains
  • Knocked Knees

Our Treatment Approach

  • To address these issues, we use a systematic approach to mobilise the bones of the foot, ankle, knee, and hip. This method is most effective when combined with orthotic therapy and a customised exercise program. Our comprehensive, holistic strategy ensures optimal outcomes for restoring proper alignment, function, and long-term relief for our patients.
