Toenail Problems

Book a Session ↗

Ingrown Toenails

Caused by improper nail trimming, tight shoes, or trauma, resulting in redness, pain, and swelling. We offer conservative care and minor procedures for long-term relief.

Fungal Toenails

Thick, brittle, discoloured nails caused by fungal infections. We provide topical treatments, laser therapy, and prevention strategies.

Nail Trauma

Injuries leading to cracked, lifted, or discoloured nails. We assess the extent of damage and provide treatment to support regrowth.

Recurring Infections

Chronic inflammation or infection of the nail and surrounding tissue. We manage these with hygiene guidance, ongoing care, and preventative advice.

Partial nail avulsion (PNA)

Ask our podiatrists about this procedure for ingrown toenails.

Onyfix nail correction

Ask at your appointment whether Onyfix nail correction is suitable for you.
