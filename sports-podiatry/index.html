Our practitioners

The Bowral Foot Clinic focuses on diagnosing, treating, and preventing foot and lower limb injuries related to physical activity and athletic performance. Whether you’re a professional athlete, weekend runner, or someone returning to exercise, sports podiatrists use biomechanical assessments, gait analysis, and evidence-based treatment strategies to help you move pain-free and avoid re-injury. 

Common sports injures we treat?

  • Plantar Fasciitis
  • Achilles Tendinopathy
  • Shin Splints
  • Stress Fractures
  • Ankle Sprains
  • Metatarsalgia
  • Sesamoiditis
  • Morton’s Neuroma
  • Iliotibial Band (ITB) Syndrome

Why Choose Bowral Foot Clinic?

At The Bowral Foot Clinic, we tailor our care to suit your sport, training load, and performance goals, getting you back to doing what you love, safely and faster.
