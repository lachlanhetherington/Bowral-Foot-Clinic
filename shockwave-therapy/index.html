Shockwave Therapy

Shockwave therapy harnesses the power of acoustic waves and compressed air to trigger a dynamic kinetic energy response, akin to a hand-held jackhammer. This cutting-edge treatment modality extends its beneficial effects to both soft and hard tissues, playing a pivotal role in facilitating repair, growth, and enhanced mobility while concurrently mitigating chronic inflammation, promoting collagen production, and releasing trigger points. Much like dry needling, our objective with shockwave therapy is to provoke an inflammatory response, a crucial mechanism for healing and recovery.

How Shockwave Therapy works?

Shockwave therapy is a versatile treatment option, offering tangible benefits for a wide spectrum of acute and chronic injuries. Our dedicated team of experts is well-versed in the application of shockwave therapy to help you achieve swift pain relief and regain optimal mobility.
