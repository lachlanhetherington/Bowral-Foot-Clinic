About Bowral Foot Clinic

Podiatry and lower-limb care for adults and children in Bowral and the Southern Highlands.

Book a Session ↗Plan your visit ↓

From Mona Road to Boolwey Street

The clinic began on Mona Road in Bowral and later moved to Boolwey Street. We continue to care for adults and children from Bowral and surrounding Southern Highlands communities.

As part of Dr. Abbie Clinics, our Bowral team provides routine foot care and lower-limb assessment. If you’re booking a biomechanical assessment, your podiatrist can look at how you stand and walk, how your footwear fits, and how your symptoms change with activity.

Your first appointment

  1. Tell us what brought you in

    Your podiatrist will ask what you’ve noticed, when it started and what you’d like help with.

  2. Have an assessment suited to your concern

    A routine visit may focus on the skin or nails. A biomechanical assessment may also include a look at the way you stand and walk.

  3. Discuss the next steps

    Your podiatrist will explain their findings and talk through the options with you.

What to bring

  • The shoes you wear most often or for the activity that bothers you.
  • Any orthotics or insoles you already use.
  • Any referral, imaging or reports relevant to your visit.
