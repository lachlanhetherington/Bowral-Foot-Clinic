Dry Needling

Soft tissues including muscles, tendons, and ligaments can often develop taut bands of tissue, commonly identified as trigger points, scar tissue, or areas with adhesions. These formations can significantly limit the range and quality of motion around joints and muscles, frequently resulting in discomfort and pain.

Needling Technique for Tissue Repair

One effective method for treating these areas involves the precise insertion of acupuncture needles into and around the taut tissue bands. This technique stimulates a muscle twitch response, as the body recognises the needle as a ‘foreign object.’ This response activates a local inflammatory process, which helps break down scar tissue. The result is improved tissue healing, enhanced mobility, and greater overall comfort for the patient.
